Required assets

two tools. both free. plus 2 to 5 minutes of your time.

NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook) Perplexity

you can experiment with a lot of other setups, but this is the easiest one, and it still gives you solid outputs.

Step 01 // pick your topic (and be greedy)
// be creative

its YOUR podcast. it can be any cross of topics. physics and finance. ai and philosophy. whatever your heart desires. so dont be shy to connect videos and topics that dont usually sit next to each other. thats where the interesting stuff is.

Step 02 // find your sources with Perplexity

go to Perplexity and run the prompt below. it gives you the best videos on your topic, plus the key subtopics in learning order, plus a clean list of urls at the end.

you have two ways to do this:

01

use your own favourite videos and sources for the topic, by hand.

02

use the videos Perplexity gives you, keep the ones you like, and enrich them with deep-researched sources in NotebookLM.

if youre looking for a specific tone or style, option 1 is better.

// why youtube?

two reasons.

most good videos have a script behind them to make the topic more interesting, which tends to increase how much you actually retain. (might be biased, but it holds up for me.)

and someone already did the research for you, in a style you comprehend. so it feels less like a lecture and more like chatting with that person.
