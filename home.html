The brief // what this is

the dossier is a public case study of self-development in progress.

i document the systems, tools, and ai workflows i test on myself across different areas of improvement. you get the real version: what i tried, what broke, what stuck, and the exact steps so you can adapt it without being technical.

its a growing archive of one person learning, building, and getting results, open to anyone who wants practical examples they can use.

Payload // what you walk away with
01
A usable system

the steps, the prompts, and the order to run them in.

02
Proof you can build your own

you watch one version get built and broken in public, so the next one is yours.

03
A different way of thinking

the reasoning behind the system, so you can change it when your situation doesnt match mine.

Method // how a file gets made
01
The subject picks itself

i only file what im already using. a problem i hit this week, a tool i actually reached for.

02
It runs on me first

long enough to break at least once. you get the version that survived, including what i had to change.

03
It gets filed

exact steps, exact prompts, in the order you run them. if you cant execute it the same day you read it, its not finished.

04
The result goes on the board

what it moved, or that it moved nothing. the mission board carries the number either way.
