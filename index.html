The problem // no one made the show you want

if youre like me you have the same problem. you want to find a podcast to listen to, but none of the topics or channels are really about what you want to learn right now.

so i stopped waiting for someone to make it. i started making my own.

this file is the whole system: take your favourite or desired topic, ground it on actual good research, and create your OWN two-person podcast for it, fully for free.
